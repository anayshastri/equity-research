The Deficit Math

Training builds and preserves muscle; the scale mostly moves in the kitchen. Here's what a 9kg loss in 12 weeks actually requires.

Total to lose
9 kg
Weekly pace
≈ 0.75 kg/week
Approx. daily deficit needed
≈ 750–850 kcal
Daily protein target
140–160 g
01
Protein first
Roughly 1.8–2.0g per kg bodyweight daily. It's the single biggest lever for keeping the muscle you're training while the scale drops.
02
Deficit from food, not from skipping training
A ~750–850 kcal/day gap gets you there in 12 weeks. Pull most of it from refined carbs and oil rather than protein or vegetables.
03
Walk daily, outside of lifting
30–40 min of brisk walking on non-lifting days adds a meaningful, low-fatigue calorie burn without eating into recovery.
04
Progress the load, not just the reps
Every 2 weeks, try to add a little weight or a rep to your top set per exercise — that's what keeps the muscle-preservation signal strong in a deficit.
05
Sleep 7+ hours
Poor sleep blunts fat loss and recovery more than almost anything else on this list — it's worth protecting like a training session.
Note: 0.75 kg/week sits at the upper edge of what's generally considered a sustainable rate of fat loss. If you're new to structured training or have any health conditions, it's worth a quick check-in with a doctor before starting — and if the pace feels too aggressive week to week, stretching the timeline is better than pushing the deficit further.
